A PRACTICAL ACTION PLAN

What to do next.

The best next step depends on what happened. Prioritise financial security and immediate safety.

01

If money was taken, act quickly

Contact your bank or payment provider using its official app or number. In India, call 1930 promptly for financial cyber fraud and report through the official portal.

02

Secure affected accounts

Change compromised passwords from a trusted device, secure your email first, enable multi-factor authentication and sign out unknown sessions.

03

Save evidence

Keep emails, screenshots, phone numbers, profile links, URLs, transaction IDs and a simple timeline. Do not edit originals if you can preserve them safely.

04

Report through official channels

Use the official cybercrime portal, follow its current instructions and save your acknowledgement details for future reference.

05

Watch for recovery scams

Be wary of people promising guaranteed refunds for an upfront fee or asking for OTPs, PINs, passwords or remote access.
