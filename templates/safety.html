COMMON WARNING SIGNS

Know the patterns scammers use.

One signal does not prove a scam. Several warning signs together mean you should verify carefully.

✉

Phishing emails

Messages imitate trusted organisations and ask you to sign in, verify details or open a link.

Watch forUnexpected account warnings or mismatched sender addresses.
↗

Suspicious links

Links can lead to lookalike websites designed to collect your login or payment information.

Watch forMisspelled domains, shortened URLs and unusual requests.
🎁

Fake rewards

Unexpected prizes or jobs may require an upfront payment or sensitive information.

Watch forFees to release a prize, job offer or refund.
⌁

Urgent payment requests

Scammers create panic to stop you from checking with the real person or organisation.

Watch forPressure to transfer money immediately or keep it secret.
♙

Credential theft

Fraudsters may ask for passwords, OTPs, PINs or remote access to your device.

Watch forRequests for authentication codes or banking PINs.
◎

Social media scams

Fake profiles, impersonation and too-good-to-be-true offers can target your trust.

Watch forNew accounts asking for money or private details.
YOUR EVERYDAY CHECKLIST

Pause. Check. Protect.

01

Think before you click

Open the official website or app directly instead of following an unexpected link.

02

Verify the sender

Contact the organisation or person using a trusted number or channel you already know.

03

Keep codes private

Never share passwords, OTPs, PINs or recovery codes with someone who contacts you.

04

Secure your accounts

Use unique passwords, multi-factor authentication and current device updates.
